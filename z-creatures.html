Alawan. Not quite a single secret place, but an ever present dimension where arcane creatures of remarkable intelligence gleefully zip around us – flying through the skies, storming through seas, slithering in shadows, and running through our streets, able to disappear in colorful shimmers of light.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

A flash of color at the corner of your eye, blinks-and-you’ll-miss movements of magical beings dismissed merely as myths and legends believed only by children. But there exist pockets of unseen worlds around us, home to enigmatic creatures called Zigbins.

Zigbins are all unique - all powerful representatives of their respective kingdoms, and each play their roles to protect their ten orbs of power, where each of their individual gods resides. These one-of-a-kind creatures are strong and powerful in a variety of ways, performing their duties according to their temperaments as fighters, healers, guardians, creators, warriors, mediators, and more while demonstrating control over all the known and unknown elements.

Legend says that only the worthy may catch them - as spirit companions and invaluable guides for life - some even able to share their powers and abilities with their masters. Although many have tried to hunt down these Zigbins for their extraordinary power, they remain elusive, with only a handful who have succeeded - their power increased and wishes granted in exchange for undying loyalty.

Discover and befriend your own Z-creature from the ten magical kingdoms of Alawan, only on Stargaze.

KINGDOM OF MAYON-AL type: fire z-creature

It is said that the god of Mayon-al was first and foremost, a fighter with a terrible temper. With power drawn from pure and undiluted rage, the fire god sped through the world, moving plates of land, cracking them into pieces according to its will.

Being of pure energy and primers of both creation and destruction, the z-god of Mayon-al sits on their volcanic throne, as the ruler of the first and primordial fire that warms the earth. Satisfied by its work, it created their own Forest of Fire, a realm hotter than any known hells where they are known to reside.

Its travels created volcanoes and rocky mountains, with hot springs and smaller fire forests decorating these haunts where their devoted followers, lesser warriors, spirits and lower Z-creatures would gather and reside.

The Zigbins of Mayon-al are fire manipulators and capable combatants. With heightened speeds, super strength, and competent shield casting, they are desirable if not fortheir rash and impulsive natures that have them rushing into the first signs of battle with only healers being able to challenge them into submission.

KINGDOM OF Tubbataha type: fire z-creature

There is only one force that can truly quell the fiery forces of nature and it is the god of Tubbataha that holds such power. Water heals in the ebb and flow, cleansing in its path, and enriching through an ever-expanding reach of rising and falling tides.

They say that there are only two things that the z-god of Tubbataha does not know: when the worlds - both the known and Alawan were created, and how the first Zigbins, the gods themselves, came to be.

But water is memory. Tubbataha entrenches itself in the expansive sea, crossing lands through river systems creating spring pools that fall and flow through forests and mountains, spilling into lakes and lagoons and back into the ocean as we see it. Ruler of the oceans, seas, lakes, and all bodies of water in all shape and form, Tubbataha in its prime, created acres of ice and coral in its attempt to recreate Bakawanara’s ability to draw lines between land and water.

The Zigbins of Tubbataha share the abilities of healing and agility to counter most types of aggression and are able to move and shape water according to their wills. Some are even able to render themselves invisible and manipulate water sources into ice as both a healing and hostile force. Despite this flexibility, Tubbataha zigbins are only as strong as their nearest source of water and are often vulnerable to the whims of the wind that dictate most of their movement.
