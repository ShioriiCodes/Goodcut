Those who answered the call and listened to the whispers, Z-Hunters are uniquely capable souls that are able to create arcane lifelong partnerships with Zigbins. Not much is known about the extremely rare Zigbins - by the very nature of the kingdoms they inhabit, they are difficult to find, and more difficult still, to catch. Zigbins are magnificent magical creatures to be caught and coddled into a lifelong companionship. This natural alliance has existed for an indeterminate time where newcomers may be fascinated by the ecosystem of magic users that are borne from these fantastical friendships.

These Z-Hunters rise through their ranks, ascending to the coveted role of a Z-Master. Ten ruling Z-Masters have often changed with the passage of time, but there can only be one master in every kingdom, working together to protect the delicate balance that is Alawan and its sister worlds.

Story

Once long ago in a time before consciousness, there was a powerful unknowable force called Adarna who fashioned the worlds and its inhabitants out of sheer force of will. In allowing life to grow and evolve with the changing climates and shifting earths, various civilizations rose made of mortals, magic-users, and magical creatures that all lived in balanced harmony.

Kingdoms rose with the times and the Adarna ordained the first Z-Masters with the extraordinary responsibility of balancing all of life. Z-Hunters developed a class system to create order in the paths to enlightenment with trials and tribulations to forge the worthy. The Adarna faded into obscurity and the worlds and its inhabitants intermingled and evolved with new and complex desires.

Where even the path to become a Z-Hunter, while not impossible, became a revered position for those who sought to tame and bond zigbins. While there are Z-Hunters that seek only to have the one magical ally for a lifetime, there is potential for the bold and ambitious to hold more than one Zigbin - and with enough loyal allies, can one be bestowed the honor of becoming a Z-Master.

Z-Masters

Of the hundreds and thousands that clamor to rise through the ranks of Z-Hunters, only those with Adarna’s mercy and favor can achieve true Z-Master status. Chosen only after one master makes a natural return to the fabrics of Alawan, an aspiring Z-Hunter must first appeal through several trials, tribulations, and terrifying scenarios to invite the attention of the all-powerful Adarna. The Adarna itself does not specify the race or origin of aspiring kingdom leaders, but rather judges them based on their morals and mettle. Z-Masters of the past have all had their share of triumphs and sacrifices - some at greater cost than they were truly ready to pay for, and it is the conscious awareness of the weight that is both power and responsibility that does not allow just anyone to ascend.
